## 1. About Us

Uztelecom Call Center LLC is a customer experience and call center service provider with proven expertise in the telecommunications sector. We combine skilled multilingual agents, modern contact center technology, and data-driven quality management to deliver measurable results for our partners.

## 2. Scope of Services

We propose to support Uztelecom with the following services:

- **Inbound customer support** — billing inquiries, technical support (Tier 1/2), plan and tariff information, complaints handling
- **Outbound campaigns** — customer retention, upselling/cross-selling of telecom packages, satisfaction surveys, debt collection reminders
- **Multichannel support** — voice, live chat, email, and messaging platforms (Telegram, WhatsApp)
- **Technical helpdesk** — troubleshooting for mobile, broadband, and IPTV services
- **Back-office support** — order processing, SIM activation/verification, ticket management
- **Quality assurance & reporting** — call monitoring, CSAT/NPS tracking, real-time dashboards, SLA compliance reports

## 4. Proposed Service Model

- **Team structure:** Dedicated account manager, team leaders, QA specialists, and trained customer service agents
- **Working hours:** 24/7 coverage or business-hours support, based on Uztelecom's requirements
- **Onboarding:** 2–4 weeks for knowledge transfer, systems integration, and agent training
- **SLA targets:** Average speed of answer, first-call resolution rate, CSAT score, and abandonment rate — to be jointly defined

## 5. Indicative Pricing

Pricing will be finalized based on call volume, channel mix, and required coverage hours. We offer flexible commercial models, including:

- **Per-agent monthly fee** (dedicated team)
- **Per-call / per-minute pricing** (shared team, variable volume)
- **Hybrid model** combining a fixed base fee with performance-based incentives

A detailed cost breakdown will be provided upon review of Uztelecom's expected volumes and service scope.
